Cyclistic Case Study – Member vs Casual Riders Analysis

Date: September 2025  |  Stack: SQL (BigQuery), R (tidyverse, ggplot2), Tableau, Python

Short Summary

This project analyzes 5.64 million Cyclistic bike trips to compare annual members and casual riders, uncovering key patterns and actionable recommendations to increase memberships and optimize fleet usage.
