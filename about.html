About Me

I'm Sheikh Muhammad Jawad Ahmed, a self-taught Data & Business Analyst who sees data not just as numbers but as stories that can transform decisions.

I began my journey with a commerce background and grew into data analytics through curiosity, consistent learning and hands-on experience with real case studies and internships.

During college, I created and ran my own e-commerce store on leading platforms and also explored app development using Flutter. It was exciting, but I realized I wanted a career where I could truly use my problem-solving, analytical and presentation skills. One day, I came across an article about data analysis and visualization. That moment sparked my interest. I started exploring data analytics and visualization, beginning with Excel to analyze small datasets and gradually advancing to more complex tools and techniques.

Later, I enrolled in the Google Data Analytics Professional Certification to learn directly from industry experts, while also applying for internships to gain practical experience.

During my first internship, I worked remotely with an international team. It was my first experience in the field and most of my teammates were already experienced, but I didn’t give up. I kept learning, completed my tasks on time and even helped others in solving problems. That experience taught me teamwork, adaptability and how to handle complex challenges under pressure. Even without a leading role, I took every opportunity to learn, contribute and support my team wherever possible.

My second internship was also remote but this time, I led an international team. My hard work and consistency paid off when I was trusted with that responsibility. It wasn’t easy managing different time zones and keeping everyone on the same track. I worked on multiple large datasets, primarily using PostgreSQL and Python, while also building a dashboard in Looker Studio. On our final day, we delivered a presentation that was well-received by the stakeholders. Leading that team taught me how to coordinate across time zones, stay organized and guide diverse members toward a shared goal.

Over time, I’ve developed strong skills in Excel, SQL, R, Python, Tableau and Power BI, which I use to clean, analyze and visualize real-world data.

My goal is to keep improving every day, unlocking hidden patterns, driving business growth and helping organizations to make smarter and data-driven decisions.
