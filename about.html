About Me

I'm Sheikh Muhammad Jawad Ahmed, a self-taught Data & Business Analyst who turns complex datasets into clear, actionable insights. Coming from a commerce background, I gradually built my expertise in data analytics through continuous learning and hands-on experience.

My learning journey began with Excel and grew into advanced tools like SQL, Python, R, Tableau and Power BI. Along the way, I completed the Google Data Analytics Professional Certification and applied my skills through hands-on projects and remote international internships.

I’ve worked with global teams across time zones, contributing to data cleaning, transformation, dashboard development and end-to-end analysis pipelines. In my most recent internship, I led an international team through a multi-dataset analytics project, delivering insights and visualizations that received strong stakeholder feedback.

I focus on uncovering patterns, simplifying complex data and presenting insights that support business growth. My goal is to keep improving every day and help organizations make smarter, data-driven decisions.
